# R-87 scripted device pass

Run once per release candidate on a physical device. The store must contain at
least two years of history from at least three sources. Public evidence names
source classes or opaque labels only; it must not contain source app names,
HealthKit values, credentials, destination hostnames, screenshots of Health, or
raw diagnostics.

Copy `result.synthetic.json` outside the repository, replace every synthetic
field, and retain each check ID. Validate it with:

```sh
python3 qa/device-pass/validate.py /private/path/device-pass.json
```

The validator checks completeness only. It cannot observe the device and does
not turn an unperformed check into a pass.

## Preconditions

1. Record device model, exact OS build, app build, operator, UTC start time,
   history span in days, source count, and non-identifying store
   characteristics.
2. Confirm history span is at least 730 days and source count is at least three.
3. Use a local archive or dedicated test destination. Keep all detailed
   comparison worksheets private and reference them by digest.

## Named checks

Execute in order and record `pass`, `fail`, or `blocked`, an evidence reference,
and a value-free note:

1. `onboarding-disclosure` — locked-device disclosure precedes Health permission.
2. `authorization-states` — one type denied; denied/limited copy is accurate.
3. `health-browser-comparison` — compare three app readings to Health privately.
4. `destination-canary` — each shipping destination passes its R-25 real-path test.
5. `destination-change-notice` — adding and re-pointing emits R-40 notices.
6. `certificate-change-halt` — changed certificate halts pending re-approval.
7. `diagnostic-second-person` — a second person diagnoses a seeded failure using
   only the redacted bundle.
8. `hidden-destination-prohibited` — hiding a destination is unavailable.
9. `statistics-comparison` — one multi-source aggregate agrees with the private
   HealthKit statistics worksheet, or a divergence issue is linked.
10. `encrypted-backup` — execute `qa/encrypted-backup/PROTOCOL.md`.
11. `delete-all` — R-43 leaves zero app Keychain items and no app container data.
12. `status-widget` — last-success age and state are understandable without values.
13. `voiceover-critical-flows` — onboarding, destination setup, and failure state.

Any `fail` or `blocked` check makes the device pass fail. Divergences require an
issue URL. Sign-off records who evaluated the evidence; it is not generated by
the script.
