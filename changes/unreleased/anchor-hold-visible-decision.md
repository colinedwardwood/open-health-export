A paused type now says so on screen and asks what to do about it (QA-17).

A banner names the paused data and stays until the hold is decided, because a dismissed
banner would leave a type quietly not exporting. The two answers are spelled out with
what each costs: send the history again, or stop exporting that data. Both are recorded
before the next run reads them, so resuming is a decision rather than the side effect of
a retry.
