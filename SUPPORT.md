# Support

Support is best-effort by volunteers. There is currently one maintainer.

**Use GitHub Issues for bugs** in the app, exporter, or wire format. Do not paste real
HealthKit values, tokens, hostnames of private servers, or crash logs that contain them.

**Use GitHub Discussions** (when enabled) or a plain question issue for "how do I…?"
questions. There is no chat, no SLA, and no paid support path.

This project does **not** support: interpreting your measurements, App Store review on
your behalf, or destinations we do not ship (Dropbox, Google Drive, iCloud as a sync
store).

If you found a vulnerability, use `SECURITY.md`. Do not open a public issue.
